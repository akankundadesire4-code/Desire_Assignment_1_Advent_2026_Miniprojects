Mini-Project 5: Taxi Route Revenue, Pricing & Fleet Planner
====================================================
Plain-language notes for questions the assignment asks us to explain.

1. Is the current UGX 2,000 Ntinda fare above or below equilibrium?
   - We modelled demand as Qd = 120 - 0.02P and supply as Qs = 10 + 0.03P.
   - At equilibrium, Qd = Qs, so we solve the 2x2 linear system
     0.02P + Q = 120 and -0.03P + Q = 10 (rearranged) with
     scipy.linalg.solve to find the equilibrium price P* and quantity Q*.
   - Compare the notebook's calculated P* to the current UGX 2,000 fare. If
     2,000 is below P*, the fare is set below market equilibrium (this can
     mean more demand than supply at that price, i.e. it may be
     under-priced for the route). If it is above P*, the fare may be
     discouraging some riders relative to what the market would otherwise
     support. See the notebook for the specific number with this data.

2. Why do we use walk-forward (rolling-origin) backtesting instead of a
   single train/test split?
   - Walk-forward backtesting re-fits (or re-applies) each forecaster at
     every day from day 4 to day 10, always using only the data available
     up to that point, then checks the very next day's real value.
   - This mimics how the forecast would really be used in production (one
     day at a time, never seeing the future), so it gives a fairer,
     less lucky/unlucky estimate of each model's typical error (MAE) than
     one single fixed split would.

3. How is the day-11 fleet size calculated?
   - Each vehicle can carry 14 passengers per trip and makes 8 one-way
     trips a day, giving a capacity of 8 * 14 = 112 passengers per vehicle
     per day.
   - We take the best model's forecast passenger count for day 11, add a
     15% safety buffer (to avoid under-supplying the route), divide by 112,
     and round up (ceiling), since you cannot deploy a fraction of a
     vehicle. This is implemented in vehicles_needed() in
     src/project5_taxi.py.
